STEP 1 - METADATA

Title:
Amyloid, social and sensory risk factors and cortical tau stage at autopsy: a pre-registered analysis of the Rush ROS and MAP cohorts

Description:
Module 3 of my project on the locus coeruleus (LC) as a rate controller of Alzheimer's progression. Using the Rush Alzheimer's Disease Center Standard dataset for the Religious Orders Study (ROS) and the Rush Memory and Aging Project (MAP), requested and not yet received, I test whether amyloid load tracks Braak neurofibrillary tangle stage at autopsy, whether amyloid acts at cortical entry (Braak stage I or above versus 0), and whether social network size, loneliness, late-life social activity and near vision go with a higher Braak stage. The dataset's Braak stage has no LC or pre-tangle stages, so it cannot test the LC to cortex step itself. Mediation through the LC is a pre-specified conditional branch, run only if RADC delivers LC measures. The full plan is in the attached PDF.

STEP 2 - OVERVIEW

Research questions or hypotheses:
Each association is a proportional-odds odds ratio per SD, oriented so that above 1 is the predicted direction. Verdicts use Bonferroni-adjusted intervals within each family (Family A: H1 and H2, 97.5% intervals; Family B: H3 for the four exposures, 98.75% intervals). Supports: adjusted interval above 1. AGAINST: below 1. AGAINST (practically null): entirely inside 0.90 to 1.11. Uninformative: none of these and wider than a 4-fold range. Otherwise inconclusive.

H1. Higher amyloid load (amylsqrt_est_8reg) goes with a higher Braak stage (braaksc), given age at death, sex, education, APOE e4 carrier status and cohort. Counts against: AGAINST or practically null, which would count against amyloid gating (Module 2, variant A). Stated in advance: H1 can hardly fail and does not discriminate LC-first from the amyloid cascade; in simulation it said supports in every world where amyloid speeds cortical spread.

H2. Higher amyloid load goes with cortical entry (Braak stage I or above versus 0), same adjustment. Counts against: AGAINST or practically null, meaning amyloid does not act at the step nearest the LC to cortex transition. If H1 supports and H2 does not, I report this as evidence against amyloid gating of the LC to cortex step. This is the discriminating test.

H3 (four tests). A smaller social network (soc_net), more loneliness (social_isolation), less late-life social activity (late_life_soc_act) and worse near vision (vision) each go with a higher Braak stage, same adjustment. Counts against: AGAINST for that exposure (association on the protective side), which counts against the premise that the factor raises tau pathology. Stated in advance: with the expected sample sizes the practically-null verdict was never reached in simulation, so a factor with no effect will read inconclusive; and a supports verdict does not show that the LC is involved.

H4 (only if LC measures are delivered; Killer 2). For each exposure, the natural indirect effect through the LC measure on expected Braak stage is positive (Family C, 4 tests, Bonferroni-adjusted bootstrap intervals). Killer 2 is met for an exposure if the adjusted interval includes 0 or lies below 0, and met overall if it is met for every exposure that can be estimated. A predicted-direction effect whose rho* is below 0.1 in absolute value is reported as fragile. Without LC measures H4 is not run and Killer 2 stays untested.

Foreknowledge of data or evidence:
Data exists but the authors cannot observe it yet.

Nothing from it has been downloaded, opened, summarised or analysed by me or by the AI model I used, and no RADC account was used by the AI model. Those pages carry no data values. ROS and MAP have been analysed in many published papers, and both I and the AI model know that literature in general terms. To limit its influence: the hypotheses and their directions come from the LC-first framework in my project brief, written before this dataset was chosen; every model, rule, threshold and sensitivity analysis is fixed in the attached plan and in config/mediation.yaml; the verdict rules were checked on simulated data only; H1, which the literature makes near-certain, is labelled non-discriminating; and the LC branch is decided by the delivered codebook and RADC's written answer, entered and committed before the data are opened. The code refuses to run until this plan is frozen and every variable name has been confirmed against the codebook.

STEP 3 - RESEARCH DESIGN

Study type:
Adjustment or control-based design
The data are observational (clinical-pathologic cohorts, no manipulation, no randomisation). Every estimate that could be read causally rests on adjustment for a confounder set fixed in a pre-specified causal diagram (section 4 of the PDF), with E-values and, for mediation, a rho sensitivity analysis for unmeasured confounding.

Intention for causal interpretation:
Indirect inference
The associations and the natural indirect effects are interpreted causally only under the stated assumptions (no unmeasured confounding given the adjustment set, assumed order of amyloid before Braak stage, and, for H4, sequential ignorability). The report states these assumptions in its abstract; Section 4 of the PDF lists them.

Blinding of experimental treatments:
No blinding is involved in this study.

Additional blinding during research or analysis:
RADC's public documentation states that the CERAD rating is made blind to age and clinical data and that the final clinical diagnosis is made blind to postmortem data; I had no role in any rating. My own analysis is not blinded, but every decision is fixed before the data are seen and the code runs only the registered analyses.

Study design:
Cross-sectional analysis of autopsy outcomes in two longitudinal cohorts (ROS and MAP). Outcome: Braak stage 0 to VI at autopsy. Exposures: amyloid load at autopsy (H1, H2) and four social and sensory variables measured in life, primary value the earliest non-missing (baseline) measurement (H3). Conditional branch: mediation through an LC measure if delivered (H4). Section 4 (causal diagram) and section 5 (methods) of the attached PDF give the details.

Randomization:
Not applicable

STEP 4 - SAMPLING

Data collection procedures:
No new data are collected. The data are the RADC Standard dataset for ROS and MAP (requested from the RADC Research Resource Sharing Hub, not yet received). Both studies enrol older people without known dementia, with annual clinical evaluation and brain donation. MARS, Clinical Core and Latino Core participants are excluded if delivered. Variable names come from RADC's public documentation and will be confirmed against the delivered codebook; the participant keys and the age at each visit are not on the public list and are marked to be confirmed. See section 3 of the PDF.

Sample size:
All ROS and MAP participants with a Braak stage (autopsied participants). The number is not known to me; I did not look it up. The MAP-only exposures (social_isolation, late_life_soc_act) use MAP participants only.

Sample size rationale:
The sample is fixed by the cohort. I measured the operating characteristics on synthetic data at 500, 1000 and 1500 autopsied participants (section 6 of the PDF). With no effects, each test said supports in at most 2% of data sets. H2 has 23% to 97% power for an odds ratio of 1.5 per SD at cortical entry, depending on sample size and on the share of brains at stage 0. H3 has 7% to 72% power for 1.15 per SD and 24% to 99% for 1.3 per SD. The LC branch detects a modest indirect effect in 46% of data sets at 300 participants with an LC measure and 99% at 800.

Starting and stopping rules:
None. The analysis is run once, after the data arrive, this plan is frozen, the validation has passed and the variable names have been confirmed.

STEP 5 - VARIABLES

Manipulated variables:
None. This is an observational study.

Measured variables:
Outcome: braaksc (Braak stage 0 to VI, silver stain); sensitivity b_score (4 levels). Exposures: amylsqrt_est_8reg (amyloid density, 8 regions); sensitivity a_score_st4 (Thal-based A score); soc_net; social_isolation (MAP only); late_life_soc_act (MAP only); vision (near acuity, 1 to 7, higher is worse). Covariates: age_death, msex, educ, apoe_genotype, cohort (study). Timing and weights: age_bl, died, dcfdx, cogn_global. Keys projid, study and fu_year are to be confirmed against the codebook. Conditional: an LC measure (priority: LC neuronal density, LC neuronal loss rating, LC tangle density), only if delivered. The table in section 3 of the PDF gives each variable's status and source page.

Indices:
APOE e4 carrier: apoe_genotype codes 24, 34 or 44. Cohort indicator: MAP versus ROS. Each exposure is standardised by its SD in the analysis sample and oriented so the predicted risk side is positive. Exposure timing: earliest non-missing value (primary); mean of all visits and last value before death (sensitivity). Age at each visit: age_bl plus fu_year unless the delivery has a visit-age column. The LC measure, if any, is z-scored and oriented so that higher means more damage.

STEP 6 - ANALYSIS PLAN

Statistical models:
H1 and H3: proportional-odds (cumulative logit) regression of Braak stage on the exposure plus age at death, sex, education, APOE e4 carrier and cohort; Wald intervals from the observed information. H2: logistic regression of Braak stage I or above versus 0 on amyloid with the same adjustment. Secondary, two-sided, no verdict: age given amyloid, the amyloid by age interaction, age in amyloid-free brains (if at least 30), and the LC by amyloid interaction in the LC branch. H4: linear model for the z-scored LC measure and ordered probit for Braak stage, without interaction; natural indirect and direct effects on expected Braak stage computed exactly for a 25th to 75th percentile exposure contrast; 2000 bootstrap replicates; rho sensitivity from -0.95 to 0.95 in steps of 0.01 with the outcome model corrected exactly at each rho. See sections 5 and 8 of the PDF.

Transformations:
Exposures standardised per SD and oriented to the predicted risk side. Braak stage used as 7 ordered levels; sensitivity analyses use 4 levels (b_score) and a numeric 0 to 6 scale. APOE genotype coded as e4 carrier. The LC measure z-scored. No other transformations; amylsqrt_est_8reg is used as delivered (already square-root transformed by RADC).

Inference criteria:
Bonferroni-adjusted intervals within families: Family A (H1, H2; 97.5%), Family B (H3, four exposures; 98.75%), Family C (H4, four exposures). Verdicts: supports (adjusted interval above 1), AGAINST (below 1), AGAINST practically null (entirely inside 0.90 to 1.11, that is |log OR| below 0.10536), uninformative (adjusted log width above 1.386), otherwise inconclusive. A supports or AGAINST verdict is robust only if sensitivity analyses S1, S2, S3, S5 and S6, where they apply, agree. E-values are reported for every confirmatory odds ratio. For H4, Killer 2 rules as in the hypotheses field, with the fragile label below 0.1 in absolute rho*.

Data inclusion and exclusion:
Included: ROS and MAP participants with a Braak stage. Excluded: other RADC cohorts. Each test uses complete cases for its own variables; every excluded participant is counted and logged with the missing variables. For the MAP-only exposures the analysis is restricted to MAP (structural absence, not missing data). No outlier exclusions.

Missing data:
Primary: complete cases per analysis, every exclusion logged. If more than 5% of eligible participants are excluded for reasons other than structural absence, sensitivity analysis S8 repeats the test with multiple imputation by chained equations (20 imputations, 10 cycles, predictive mean matching), pooled by Rubin's rules.

Other planned analysis:
Sensitivity analyses S1 to S10: Thal A score instead of amyloid density; 4-level Braak score; Braak stage as a number with linear regression and robust errors; exposure as mean of all visits and as last value; exposures measured at a visit with no cognitive impairment; exposures measured at least 5 years before death; H3 additionally adjusted for amyloid; multiple imputation if triggered; inverse-probability-of-autopsy weights (truncated at the 99th percentile, 500 bootstrap replicates); ROS and MAP separately. For H4: the same decomposition with linear models and the closed-form rho. Validation that must pass before any real analysis: reproduction of Imai, Keele and Tingley (2010) Table 1 and of Imai, Keele and Yamamoto (2010) Section 6.2 from the authors' public replication data, plus simulation checks of every estimator (section 7 of the PDF).
